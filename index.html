Questions

Frequently asked

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to my account (OPay/PalmPay also work). If you prefer to pay by card, I can send a secure payment link. I take a 50% deposit to start and the balance before your site goes live.

Do I need to pay for hosting every year?

No. Your site is hosted on a free, reliable platform. The only optional extra is your own domain name (like yourbusiness.com.ng), which you pay to the registrar yearly, registered in your name.

Can I start without a domain name?

Yes. Your site can launch on a free web address first, and you can add your own domain later whenever you're ready.

How long does it take?

A Starter One-Page usually takes about 5–7 days after I receive your photos and details. A Business Site takes about 1–2 weeks.

Can customers pay on my website?

Yes. I can add a "Pay now" link using Paystack or Flutterwave. Those services charge a small fee per payment, which I'll explain clearly before setting it up.

What if I need changes later?

Monthly Upkeep covers regular edits like new prices, photos and banners. Without upkeep, I quote small changes one at a time.
